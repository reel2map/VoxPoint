About the penthouse

{{ property.tagline }}.

Located at {{ property.address.street }}, in the heart of Florence's historic centre, the F1 Penthouse puts you within walking distance of the city's greatest landmarks. The Arno river is a 1-minute walk. Ponte Vecchio takes 8 minutes on foot. The Uffizi Gallery is 9 minutes away.

The Ferrari theme runs throughout — from the entrance through to the living areas — with authentic memorabilia and design details that reflect decades of motorsport history. Guests consistently describe it as tasteful rather than kitschy.

Two private terraces give you city and rooftop views. The fully equipped kitchen means you can cook in. And with 3 bedrooms, 8 beds, and 3 full bathrooms, there's genuine comfort for up to 7 guests.
