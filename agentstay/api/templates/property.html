About the suite

La Corsa Suite Firenze is a fully refurbished penthouse at Via dei Federighi 1, 50 metres from Goldoni Square and 7–10 minutes' walk from Santa Maria Novella station. Ponte Vecchio, the Uffizi Gallery, and the Duomo are all within a 10-minute walk — yet the street itself is quiet and residential.

The suite combines high-tech interiors with authentic Ferrari heritage: original pieces and memorabilia that invite you to share a genuine passion for Italian motorsport, rather than mere decoration. The result is a space that feels like a private gallery as much as a home.

Two magnificent terraces overlook the Florentine hills. Make a BBQ, relax in the jacuzzi (available seasonally, price on request), and dine outdoors under the Tuscan sky. The outdoor kitchen — with oven, sink, and BBQ — makes al fresco living effortless.

The building is protected by Belle Arti and has no lift — but we provide a luggage porter service included in the price. Check-in is always personal: your host meets you at the property. A private Italian chef, with a culinary tradition going back 150 years, is available on request to cook in your kitchen or arrange a bespoke dining experience.
